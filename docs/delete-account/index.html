Vehicle Care — Delete Your Account & Data

Effective date: August 28, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Vehicle Care account and the data associated with it. See also our Privacy Policy for what we collect and why.

How to request deletion

Send a deletion request by email from the address you signed in with:

medhat.mmh5@gmail.com
Subject: "Delete my Vehicle Care account"

We verify the request is coming from your own account's email address, then process it — normally within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email, display name, sign-in credentials).
  • Every vehicle stored on your account, and its full history: service and maintenance records, fuel fill-ups, expenses.
  • Any vehicle photos you uploaded while signed in.

What is not affected

  • Data that only ever lived on your device — including your saved parking location, which is never uploaded to our servers in the first place — is unaffected by an account deletion request. Uninstalling the app removes it, or you can clear it from within the app at any time.
  • Crash diagnostics already sent to Firebase Crashlytics before your request are not tied to your account identity and are retained under Firebase's own standard retention period, after which they age out automatically.
Deleting your account is permanent. Your vehicles and their full service, fuel and expense history cannot be recovered once deletion is processed. If you only want to stop syncing, sign out instead — your data stays on this device.

Deleting only some of your data

You do not have to delete your whole account to remove data:

  • In the app — open any vehicle and use its delete option to remove that vehicle and its full history (service records, fuel fill-ups, expenses, photos) immediately, while keeping your account and every other vehicle.
  • By email — send the same request above naming what to remove (for example, a specific vehicle, or your photos only) and we will delete just that, leaving the rest of your account intact.

You can also export your data first (PDF, CSV or JSON) from the app's export screen before requesting deletion.
