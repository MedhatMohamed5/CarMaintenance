Vehicle Care — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026 · last updated for app version 1.0.4

Vehicle Care ("the app", "we", "our") is a vehicle maintenance, fuel and expense tracker. This page explains what information the app collects, how it is used and stored, and the choices you have. The app is fully usable without creating an account — signing in is only needed if you want your data to sync across devices.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Account information (only if you sign in)

If you choose to sign in with Email/Password or Google, we receive your email address, display name and profile photo (Google sign-in only) through Firebase Authentication. Signing in is never required to use the app.

1.2 Vehicle, maintenance, fuel and expense data

Everything you enter yourself: vehicle details (make, model, odometer reading, purchase date, license and insurance expiry), service and maintenance records, booked service appointments, fuel fill-ups, expenses, your own notes, and any workshops you add. This data is stored on your device at all times, and additionally synced to our Firebase Firestore database only while you are signed in.

1.3 Photos: vehicles, invoices and receipts

If you attach a photo to a vehicle, or photograph an invoice or receipt for a service or an expense, the app requests camera or photo library access to capture or pick it. These images are stored inside the record they belong to, and are treated exactly like the rest of your vehicle data — kept on your device, and synced to Firestore only while you are signed in.

An invoice you photograph is stored as you took it. We do not read, scan or extract text from it, but please be aware that a receipt may itself show a workshop's details, prices or a name — whatever the document contains is what gets stored.

1.4 Location

The app asks for location access for two separate, optional features, and they are stored differently. Nothing here happens in the background: your location is only read while one of these screens is open.

  • Save my parking spot — records where you left the car. This location is stored only on your device; it is never uploaded to our servers or synced to the cloud, even when you are signed in.
  • Pinning a workshop on the map — when you add or edit a workshop you can place it on a map, and optionally centre that map on where you currently are. The coordinates you save become part of that workshop's record, so like the rest of your data they are synced to Firestore while you are signed in. What is stored is a place you chose, not a trace of where you have been.

Map tiles are loaded directly from OpenStreetMap, so opening the map sends your device's IP address and the map area you are viewing to the OpenStreetMap Foundation, under their privacy policy. We send them nothing else, and no other screen in the app loads a map.

1.5 Diagnostics and crash reports

We use Sentry to learn about crashes and errors, so we can fix them. Earlier versions of the app used Firebase Crashlytics for this; it has been removed.

A report contains the device model, OS version, app version and the technical details of the failure (stack trace). On Android it may also include the operating system's own record of a native crash, which lists what every thread in the app was doing at that moment. It does not include your vehicle, maintenance, fuel, expense or note data, and it does not include your photos or invoices.

We have switched off Sentry's collection of the device IP address. If you are signed in, a report is tagged with your account's internal user ID — not your email and not your name — so that one driver hitting the same crash repeatedly reads as one problem rather than several. Performance tracing and session replay are switched off entirely.

1.6 Remotely published content

Fuel prices, the starting workshop directory and the support hotline are published by us through Firebase Remote Config, so they can be corrected without shipping an update. That is a download, not an upload: fetching it sends the app version, the device language and a Firebase installation identifier, and none of your vehicle or account data.

2. What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad networks — the app has no ads.
  • No analytics, attribution or tracking SDKs. Sentry is used for crash diagnostics only, and is the only third-party SDK that receives anything about your use of the app.
  • No background location, no location history, no profiling.
  • We never sell your data.
  • We never share your data with third parties. The only companies that handle any of it on our behalf are the infrastructure providers named here — Google (Firebase) and Sentry — acting strictly as data processors.

4. Permissions the app requests, and why

  • Camera — to photograph a vehicle, or an invoice or receipt you want to keep with a record.
  • Location — for two optional features only: saving your parking spot (kept on your device) and centring the map on you while you pin a workshop. Never read in the background.
  • Notifications — to remind you about upcoming services, worn parts and document renewals. Reminders are scheduled and shown entirely on your device.
  • Ignore battery optimisations — offered, never required. Some Android manufacturers stop a backgrounded app from running its scheduled reminders at all; this is the system setting that exempts them, and the app only opens that dialog when you ask it to from the settings screen.
  • Storage — to save exported reports (PDF/CSV/JSON) you generate yourself, to your device's Downloads folder.

5. Your choices

  • You can use the entire app without ever creating an account.
  • You can delete any vehicle, and its history, from inside the app at any time. Individual records, photos and attached invoices can be deleted the same way.
  • You can refuse the camera and location permissions, or revoke them later in your device settings; every other part of the app keeps working.
  • You can export your data (PDF, CSV or JSON) at any time from the app's export screen.
  • To request deletion of your account and all associated cloud data, see how to delete your account.

6. Data retention

Local data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Cloud data is retained for as long as your account exists, and deleted upon a verified deletion request.

7. Children's privacy

Vehicle Care is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
