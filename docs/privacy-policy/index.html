Vehicle Care — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 28, 2026

Vehicle Care ("the app", "we", "our") is a vehicle maintenance, fuel and expense tracker. This page explains what information the app collects, how it is used and stored, and the choices you have. The app is fully usable without creating an account — signing in is only needed if you want your data to sync across devices.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Account information (only if you sign in)

If you choose to sign in with Email/Password or Google, we receive your email address, display name and profile photo (Google sign-in only) through Firebase Authentication. Signing in is never required to use the app.

1.2 Vehicle, maintenance, fuel and expense data

Everything you enter yourself: vehicle details (make, model, odometer reading, purchase date, license and insurance expiry), service and maintenance records, fuel fill-ups, and expenses. This data is stored on your device at all times, and additionally synced to our Firebase Firestore database only while you are signed in.

1.3 Vehicle photos

If you attach a photo to a vehicle, the app requests camera or photo library access to capture or pick it. Photos are stored the same way as the rest of your vehicle data.

1.4 Location (Parking feature only)

If you use the "save my parking spot" feature, the app requests location access to record where you parked. This location is stored only on your device — it is never uploaded to our servers or synced to the cloud, even when you are signed in.

1.5 Diagnostics and crash reports

We use Firebase Crashlytics to learn about crashes and errors, so we can fix them. This includes device model, OS version, app version, and the technical details of the crash (stack trace). It does not include your vehicle, maintenance, fuel or expense data.

2. What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad networks — the app has no ads.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs beyond Firebase Crashlytics.
  • We never sell your data.
  • We never share your data with third parties, other than the infrastructure providers below acting strictly as data processors.

Cloud data is scoped to your own account by Firestore security rules — no other user or account can read or write it. Our infrastructure runs on Google Cloud / Firebase, which processes data on our behalf under its own security and compliance commitments.

4. Permissions the app requests, and why

  • Camera — to photograph a vehicle.
  • Location — only for the optional "save my parking spot" feature; stored locally only.
  • Notifications — to remind you about upcoming services, worn parts and document renewals. Reminders are scheduled and shown entirely on your device.
  • Storage — to save exported reports (PDF/CSV/JSON) you generate yourself, to your device's Downloads folder.

5. Your choices

  • You can use the entire app without ever creating an account.
  • You can delete any vehicle, and its history, from inside the app at any time.
  • You can export your data (PDF, CSV or JSON) at any time from the app's export screen.
  • To request deletion of your account and all associated cloud data, contact us using the email below.

6. Data retention

Local data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Cloud data is retained for as long as your account exists, and deleted upon a verified deletion request.

7. Children's privacy

Vehicle Care is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
